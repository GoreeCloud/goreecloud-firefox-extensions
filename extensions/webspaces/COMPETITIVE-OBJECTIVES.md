# GoreeCloud Webspaces — Competitive Objectives

GoreeCloud Webspaces may learn from established browser container/session-isolation products, including Firefox Multi-Account Containers, while maintaining an independent GoreeCloud product architecture and identity.

Objectives include:

- Match reliable browser-state separation supported by Firefox contextual identities.
- Make automatic routing deterministic and user-understandable.
- Improve multi-account workflows without copying proprietary code, assets, trade dress, or product identity.
- Keep core configuration local-first and usable without a GoreeCloud account.
- Apply least-privilege extension permissions and explicit platform-system acceptance boundaries.
- Make user overrides and exceptions first-class rather than hidden behavior.
- Preserve portability by keeping the Webspace product model distinct from one browser-internal representation.
